# Credits

Author-level attribution transcribed from the `Credits.txt` shipped in the v1.0.0 archive.

## Scope

- This is **not** a file-by-file manifest. The pack records author-level credits, not which `.xfbin` change belongs to which person. The repository does not invent that mapping.
- Each mod remains under its author's terms. Inclusion here is attribution, not a redistribution permission or a license grant.
- Links are the authors' published pages as supplied in `Credits.txt`; a missing link means the archive supplied no URL.
- If you are an author and want an entry corrected, linked, or removed, open an issue at <https://github.com/SenjuWoo/naruto-storm4-modpack/issues>.
